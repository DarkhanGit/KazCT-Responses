**Registration update 2 (4 November 2026) — selection of the machine-translation system.** The registration named Google Translate, NLLB-200-distilled-600M and Yandex Translate as candidates. Before the pilot, the two cloud services were excluded: sending response texts to a third-party service would contradict the consent statement, which told participants that only researchers would read their responses. Only systems running locally on the project's laptop were compared. Pilot: 50 Kazakh responses (10 per prompt, sampled with seed 11 among responses of at least 60 characters) translated into Russian by a member of the project team fluent in Kazakh and Russian (M. A. Sambetbayeva, senior researcher), without online translation services or AI tools following written rules (close to the source; no correction of content or style); before scoring, gender alternatives such as «(а)» and one duplicated variant were removed from the reference (change log kept by the research team). System: facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M (revision f8d333a098d19b4fd9a8b18f94170487ad3f821d), beam size 4, run with Transformers 5.21.0, in two modes: whole response and sentence by sentence. A first run showed a repetition loop in one sentence-level output; a no-repeat n-gram size of 4 was added and the pilot re-run before selection (both runs are in the project files). Final pilot results (sacreBLEU corpus scores against the human reference, n = 50): sentence-level chrF 58.95, BLEU 29.47, TER 63.08; whole-response chrF 48.00, BLEU 21.11, TER 70.88 (first run: 57.44 and 47.06). Whole-response translation dropped sentences in 4 of 50 responses. **Sentence-level NLLB-200-distilled-600M with this configuration is the machine-translation system for the translate-test and translate-train conditions**, in both directions. Machine translations contain the response texts and are shared only under the restricted-access tier. As of this update no model has been trained or evaluated on machine-translated data.
